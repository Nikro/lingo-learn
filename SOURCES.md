# Spanish Learning App - Source Tree

This document catalogs the sources used to build the "Ultimate Curriculum" for the Spanish learning app.
Sources are rated on a scale of 1-10 based on:
- **Pedagogical Soundness:** Alignment with CEFR standards.
- **Curriculum Depth:** Coverage of grammar, vocabulary, and functions.
- **Data Quality:** How well-structured the information is for an app.

## 1. Core Curriculum (CEFR & Academic)
These sources define the "What to teach" at each level.

1.  **Plan Curricular del Instituto Cervantes (CVC)**
    *   **URL:** https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/
    *   **Rating:** 10/10
    *   **Notes:** The gold standard. Official framework for teaching Spanish. Detailed breakdown of functions, notions, and grammar per level.
2.  **University of Zaragoza (UNIZAR) - B1 Program**
    *   **URL:** https://cursosdeespanol.unizar.es/sites/cursosdeespanol/files/archivos/programacion_de_nivel_b1_celeuz.pdf
    *   **Rating:** 9/10
    *   **Notes:** Excellent functional grammar breakdown. Great for understanding how to split grammar into "functions" (e.g., "expressing wishes").
3.  **Granada CLM Curriculum**
    *   **URL:** https://docsclm.clm-granada.com/documentacion_gap/Curriculum/CURRICULUM_GENERAL/Curriculum_por_COMPETENCIAS_Y_PERFIL_NIVELES_(V.inglesa).pdf
    *   **Rating:** 9/10
    *   **Notes:** Uses the "branching approach" (A1.1, A1.2, A2.1...). This matches our desired stage-based structure perfectly.
4.  **University of Barcelona (UB) - A2.2 & B2.2**
    *   **URL:** https://www.eh.ub.edu/en/spanish-language-course/level-a2-2/
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Very detailed grammar contents. Good for specific topics like "Spelling II" and "Grammar contents".
5.  **University of UNED (Madrid) - A2 Basic**
    *   **URL:** https://www.uned.es/universidad/pdf/GuiasAsignaturasCuid/PDFGuiaLibre?codigoAsignatura=04910015&curso=2024&language=en
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Solid standard curriculum for A2. Good list of lexical contents (vacations, geography, animals).
6.  **University of Granada (UGR) - B2 Program**
    *   **URL:** https://cursosdeespanol.unizar.es/sites/cursosdeespanol/files/archivos/programacion_general_de_b2.pdf
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Great for understanding the jump to B2 (arguments, complex texts, subjunctive).

## 2. Vocabulary & Lexical Resources
These sources define the "Words to teach".

7.  **Meyster Frequency Lists (Top 1000)**
    *   **URL:** https://meyster.app/frequency-lists/es/top-1000
    *   **Rating:** 9/10
    *   **Notes:** Data-driven. Shows exactly which words appear most often (de, ella, que, el, ser...). Essential for prioritizing A1 content.
8.  **iTalki Spanish A1 Vocabulary List**
    *   **URL:** https://www.italki.com/en/blog/spanish-a1-vocabulary-list
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Categorizes A1 into 12 categories (Greetings, Numbers, Time, Family, etc.). Good for structuring our "Mega Chapters".
9.  **MeloLingua (230+ Essential Words)**
    *   **URL:** https://melolingua.com/spanish-words
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Focuses on high-frequency words for A1-A2. Good for "Starter Pack" content.

## 3. Web Platforms & UI/UX Inspiration
These sources define the "How to teach" and user experience.

10. **StudySpanish.com**
    *   **URL:** https://studyspanish.com/
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Traditional grammar-heavy approach. Good for "Theory" sections. 108 units of grammar.
11. **iEspanol.org**
    *   **URL:** https://iespanol.org/spanish-a1-course/
    *   **Rating:** 8/10
    *   **Notes:** Uses a "Module" structure (37 lessons). Good for "Stages" concept. Focuses on practical dialogues.
12. **Duolingo (Course Tree)**
    *   **URL:** (General Knowledge)
    *   **Rating:** 9/10
    *   **Notes:** Best-in-class gamification and stage progression. We'll mimic their "Skill Tree" logic but with better pedagogical depth.
